#4471 · three problems, not one

owner CasterShell open reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

#4471 was merged as one issue and marked known: “the parser reads dates month first, and our tenants write the day first.” That is true for most of its reports, not all. Read by tenant and by what each report says, #4471 holds three problems. Each has its own ticket.

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October: the parser is fixed, and the booking-v3 hint won't be fixed because the form is retired. Nobody has looked at the one-day moves. Closing #4471 does not close that problem.

The split

Counted the way the desk’s view counts: each report where it is filed, by tenant. The one-day moves are filed under the parser and listed again on their own row.

  • parser. The shared parser read every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. 7 April became 4 July. Fixed: the parser now reads dd/mm for these locales.
  • hint. The booking-v3 form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Clients typed what it said, and the form read the date day first. The parser fix never reached these. The form is retired, so no new reports come in this way.
  • oneDay. The date moves by one day: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” No day/month flip does that, so neither change above fixes it. The desk noted these reports in desk-notes.json and filed them under the parser by tenant.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Intake tags guess. intakeTag comes from the reporter’s browser. The cause comes from the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run (causes.ts). Counting by tag gives the wrong size for the hint problem.
  • The cause table files the one-day moves under the parser. It footnotes them, so they read as part of the parser bug and close with it.
  • Merging hides the rest. One ticket with one count reads as one bug. The table the desk already had, for the same ticket:
