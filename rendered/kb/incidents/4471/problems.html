#4471 · three problems, not one

owner CasterShell open reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

#4471 was merged as one issue and marked known: “the parser reads dates month first, and our tenants write the day first.” That is true for most of its reports, not all. Read by tenant and by what each report says, #4471 holds three problems. Each now has its own ticket.

The fix queued for #4471 fixes the parser only. The other two problems stay after it ships.

The split

  • parser. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. 7 April becomes 4 July.
  • hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Clients type what it says, and the form reads the date day first. The parser never sees the flip, so its fix cannot reach these.
  • oneDay. The date moves by one day: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” No day/month flip does that. The desk noted these reports in desk-notes.json and filed them under the parser anyway.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Intake tags guess. intakeTag comes from the reporter’s browser. The cause comes from the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run (causes.ts). Counting by tag gives the wrong size for the hint problem.
  • The cause table counts the one-day moves as parser. It footnotes them and adds them to the parser row, so the table showed two causes where there are three.
  • Merging hides the rest. One ticket with one count reads as one bug. The table the desk already had, for the same ticket:

For the desk

  • Close #4471 against the parser fix only. Track the hint and one-day problems on their own tickets.
  • Before merging a report into a big ticket, check its tenant and channel, not its tag.
  • A report that does not read as a flip is not a duplicate, even with the same subject.
